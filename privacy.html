Privacy Policy 隱私權政策

Last updated: 2026-11-27

1. Who we are

OpenClaw Gmail ("the App") is a personal, single-user tool. Its only user is the owner of the Google account hkj0516@gmail.com. The App is not distributed to or used by anyone else.

2. Data the App accesses

With the owner's explicit OAuth consent, the App may access the owner's own Google user data through these scopes: Gmail (read, label, modify messages and basic settings), Google Drive, Google Calendar and Google Sheets.

3. How the data is used

The data is used only to provide features the owner requests from their own AI assistant, such as summarizing, searching, labeling and drafting emails, and reading or updating the owner's documents, calendar and spreadsheets.

4. Storage and security

OAuth tokens and any cached data are stored only on the owner's private home server. Data is not sold, rented or shared with third parties, and is not used for advertising. Content may be sent to the AI model provider selected by the owner solely to perform the owner's request.

5. Google API Services User Data Policy

The App's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google Workspace API data is not used to develop, improve or train generalized AI or machine-learning models.

6. Revoking access and deletion

The owner can revoke access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access stops all further data access; locally stored tokens are then deleted.
