OpenClaw Gmail

OpenClaw Gmail is a private, single-user automation tool operated by its owner (hkj0516@gmail.com) on a personal home server. It lets the owner's own AI assistant read and organize the owner's Gmail, Google Drive, Google Calendar and Google Sheets data. It is not offered to the public.
